## Let's Work Together

I speak at developer conferences, meetups, and corporate events on topics spanning graph databases and AI, CI/CD pipelines, cloud-native development, and building developer ecosystems. Whether it's a keynote, workshop, panel, or podcast, I bring a practitioner's perspective grounded in real engineering and business impact.

## Topics

### Graph Databases & Knowledge Graphs
How graph databases unlock relationship-centric insights — from fraud detection and recommendation engines to AI knowledge graphs and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Real patterns, real queries, real impact.

### CI/CD & Developer Pipelines
Modern approaches to continuous integration and delivery, including GitHub Actions, GitOps with Kubernetes, and programmable pipelines with Dagger. Moving from scripts to systems.

### Cloud-Native & Container Ecosystems
Kubernetes, CNCF projects, containerization, and building internal developer platforms that engineering teams actually love to use.

### Developer Relations & Ecosystem Building
How to build thriving developer communities, partner programs, and technical initiatives that drive adoption at scale — from zero to enterprise. Lessons from Neo4j, Dagger, GitHub, and beyond.
